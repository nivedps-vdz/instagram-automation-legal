User Data Deletion Instructions

Service: Instagram Comment Automation
Operated by: Vestadz
Effective date: 22 October 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of personal information processed by Vestadz's Instagram Comment Automation App (“App”).

Before publishing: Replace the contact placeholder with an email address you monitor. Set up a real process to find and delete applicable information in n8n execution history, logs, and any other storage you control.

How to request deletion

  1. Email info@vestadz.com with the subject line Instagram App Data Deletion Request.
  2. In your request, provide your Instagram username and enough context to help us locate the relevant comment or data. Do not send your Instagram password, access token, or other login credentials.
  3. We may ask for reasonable additional information to verify the request and avoid deleting the wrong person's information.
  4. After verification, we will review the data within our control and delete or anonymize applicable information unless retention is required or permitted by applicable law.
  5. We will respond to confirm the outcome or explain if any information must be retained and why.

What data may be covered

A request may cover Instagram comment IDs, comment text, usernames or identifiers received in webhook events, and related workflow execution records or logs under our control. The exact information depends on what was processed and retained by the App.

Revoke the App's access

You can also remove or revoke the App's access through the relevant Instagram account settings under Apps and websites or the connected-app permissions area, where available. Revoking access helps stop future authorized access but does not, by itself, delete information already stored by the App. Use the email process above to request deletion of stored information.

Processing time

We will review verified requests and respond within a reasonable period, subject to applicable legal requirements. If a request is complex or we need additional information, we will let you know.

This Privacy Policy explains how Vestadz (“we”, “us”, or “our”) handles information when operating our Instagram comment automation application (the “App”). The App is intended to receive eligible Instagram comment events and help publish automated replies on an Instagram professional account connected by an authorized account holder.

Information we process

Depending on the features enabled and the event delivered by Meta, the App may process:

  • Instagram comment identifiers and the related media identifier;
  • comment text and the commenting account's username or other identifiers included in the event;
  • webhook event metadata needed to identify and process a comment;
  • technical request and execution information generated by the automation platform.

We do not ask for Instagram passwords. Access to Instagram is provided through Meta-authorized access tokens and permissions.

How we use information

We use the information described above only as needed to receive and process eligible comment events, prepare or select an automated reply, publish a reply when authorized, troubleshoot failures, protect the App, and maintain its operation.

Legal basis and permissions

The App uses Meta/Instagram APIs and permissions authorized by the Instagram account holder. The account holder can manage or revoke the App's access through the relevant Instagram or Meta settings. Revoking access may stop the App from working.

Storage and retention

Information may be processed by n8n and the infrastructure used to host or operate the workflow. Depending on the configuration, webhook payloads and execution data may appear in workflow execution history or technical logs. We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, troubleshooting, security, and any applicable legal obligations, and then delete or anonymize it where feasible.
